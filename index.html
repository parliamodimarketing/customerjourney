Journey Builder

Trace a purchase. Find the brand.

Every purchase is a journey: part physical, part digital. Each moment where you meet the brand is a touchpoint. Add them all up — that's the brand. Start with one purchase: yours, or your customer's.

Look at its website, its ads, what the salesperson said. Is the brand yours? Write the experience you want to design. At the end you'll compare it with the experience that was actually lived.

Six stops. Trigger · Search · Compare · Buy · Receive & use · After. Not every journey uses all six, and real journeys loop back. Fill in what actually happened, not what should have.
